## Resolution: no semantic contradiction, but no technical pass

The Root Recognition Gate explicitly permits an **explicit environment assumption** as a possible recognition basis. Therefore Path A is not inherently inconsistent with the existing architecture if it remains a narrow, declared, conditional premise.

That compatibility does not mean the gate has passed. Three different statements must remain distinct:

1. **Owner-level assumption acceptance:** Kevin may explicitly decide to accept a clearly disclosed, bounded commissioning risk envelope. He has authorized evaluation only; this acceptance has not occurred.
2. **Technical evidence/recognition:** a verifier must have a justified basis to validate the exact action, Constitution and context, freshness, lifecycle and scope. The repository does not currently establish that basis.
3. **Protected enforcement:** every relevant privileged/production path must enforce the resulting decision. No deployment evidence currently proves that enforcement boundary.

An explicit owner assumption can define the intended risk model; it cannot be reported as independent technical evidence that the assumption is true. If the requirement is to resist a compromised commissioning device/OS or deceptive presentation, Path A alone is insufficient and cannot be made sufficient by stronger biometrics, hashes, signatures, or extra local layers.

## Smallest unresolved prerequisite

The earliest blocker is not another abstract contract. It is the absence of a justified protected recognition/verification boundary for this deployment, together with the still-unaccepted Path A assumption set.

Before any implementation or commissioning:
- A separate explicit owner decision would be required to accept or reject the exact disclosed Path A assumptions. “Continue” or authorization to evaluate does not constitute acceptance.
- The design must then identify how the verifier's legitimacy, exact-content/action binding, freshness and one-time consumption, lifecycle/currentness, bounded scope, provenance, and enforcement are actually established under that declared threat model.
- If those properties cannot be established without circular reliance on the candidate Nexo instance, return UNKNOWN/STOP and use an independently grounded basis or remain uncommissioned.
