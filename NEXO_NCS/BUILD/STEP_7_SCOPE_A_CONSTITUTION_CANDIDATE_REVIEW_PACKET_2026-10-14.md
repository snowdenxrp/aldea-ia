## Scope A claim and limits

The only accepted initial work objective remains:

“Nexo can record and later report the identity/reference of a specifically reviewed constitutional candidate, while continuing to treat its legitimacy and currentness as unestablished.”

A reference to this draft, if eventually recorded, would mean only “this exact candidate was the object of a review event/decision.” It would not mean:
- the draft is accepted as the final Constitution;
- any OPEN clause is operative;
- the candidate is canonical, current, legitimate, or authorized;
- Genesis trust or Constitution Authority Context has been established;
- a mission, `executeAction()`, external action, or protected effect is authorized.

## Human-review binding requirements (not implemented)

Before a reference could truthfully be recorded as “reviewed,” the review process must be able to show:
1. the complete human-readable content was presented, not merely this summary or a digest;
2. the content and stated scope were the exact content intended for review;
3. truncation, changed content, changed scope, or ambiguous presentation invalidates that review attempt;
4. the owner decision records exactly what was reviewed and what that decision means;
5. the record does not elevate the draft or the review into canonical authority/currentness.

The owner’s bounded assumption that the POCO screen faithfully presents content during a first recognition ceremony applies only to that ceremony. It is not technical proof, does not imply that a ceremony occurred, and does not establish permanent trust in the device or app.
