Date: 2026-10-14

## Purpose and boundary

This map converts the existing MASTER and already-recorded AB/P/P112 findings into a reviewable clause inventory for a future constitutional draft. It is not the Constitution, does not establish a trust root, does not resolve owner-reserved governance choices, and grants no runtime authority.

Labels:
- **RECOVERED** — principle already stated in the existing normative architecture or consolidated NCS evidence; carry it forward without silently changing meaning.
- **EXTENSION** — a precise formulation needed to make an existing principle reviewable at constitutional level; it remains a proposal until reviewed.
- **OPEN** — a material governance or implementation premise is not established by inspected evidence; do not fill it by inference.
- **CONFLICT** — reserved for a demonstrated contradiction between sources. No conflict is asserted merely because evidence is absent.
