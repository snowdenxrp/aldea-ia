# Nexo AB104.567 — Recovery from permanently blocked JOINT_ACTIVE

Date: 2026-09-27
Status: RESEARCH COMPLETE — DESIGN INPUT ONLY

## Finding
A permanently blocked JOINT_ACTIVE state cannot safely be escaped by lowering quorum or asking compromised old witnesses to authorize their own removal. Byzantine reconfiguration research treats replacement of faulty replicas as a dedicated problem and includes mechanisms that make superseded or compromised configurations harmless. A separate recovery consensus instance is also a concrete pattern for deciding recovery state before normal operation resumes. citeturn0search0turn0search28turn0search4

## Candidate recovery sequence
1. STOP protected effects.
2. Freeze JOINT_ACTIVE authority changes except the predefined recovery transition.
3. Preserve certificates, equivocation evidence, snapshots and continuity anchors.
4. Establish the latest independently justified continuity boundary.
5. Determine whether the joint configuration still satisfies its safety assumptions.
6. If yes, complete normal joint transition.
7. If no, invoke separately defined recovery authority.
8. Bind the successor to the last trusted continuity anchor and explicitly fence the compromised configuration.
9. Activate successor only after required state/continuity evidence exists.
10. Reconcile protected external effects whose status became UNKNOWN.
11. Release normal authority only after the recovery boundary is durably committed and auditable.

## Independence rule
Recovery authority must not derive solely from the witnesses whose compromise caused the blockage. A new key from the same compromised trust domain is not a new trust basis.

NEW_RECOVERY_KEY != NEW_TRUST_BASIS
NEW_WITNESS_SET != INDEPENDENT_RECOVERY_AUTHORITY
BACKUP_PRESENT != CURRENT_AUTHORITY

If no independent recovery basis exists, remain UNKNOWN/QUARANTINED.

## Byzantine behavior
Old witnesses may withhold signatures, equivocate, replay old certificates, claim activation occurred, or provide divergent snapshots. None alone selects the correct successor. Recovery uses protected continuity evidence plus the predefined recovery authority. Forward-secure approaches are relevant because superseded configurations can otherwise retain signing capability. citeturn0search0turn0search28

## Liveness boundary
A blocked transition is a liveness failure; it does not authorize weakening safety assumptions. Recovery may require quarantine/offline operation until sufficient independent evidence exists.

## Nexo consequence
Add an explicit RECOVERY_RECONFIGURATION transition distinct from ordinary MEMBERSHIP_RECONFIGURATION. Bind RecoveryAuthority, PreviousContinuityAnchor, OldWitnessEpoch, NewWitnessEpoch, NewMembershipDigest, RecoveryGeneration, StateDigest, FenceEpoch, DependencySnapshot and EvidenceSet.

Recovery cannot rewrite history or erase old certificates. It creates a new authority boundary and fences the old one.
