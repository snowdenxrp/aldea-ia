# Nexo AB104.564 — Epoch rollover and stale certificate races

## Evidence
HotStuff uses views/locks and requires the highest safe certificate to justify progress; durable storage of the highest locked QC and view is safety-critical across crash recovery. ## Race model
Consider old certificate C0 from epoch E0 and successor configuration C1 from E1:
1. C0 is presented.
2. E1 is prepared.
3. E1 activation commits, or becomes ambiguous.
4. C0 is presented again or a delayed message arrives.
5. System crashes at any boundary.

Safe decisions depend on the durable linearization point of epoch activation, not arrival time.

## Candidate states
E0_ACTIVE -> E1_PREPARED -> E1_COMMITTING -> E1_ACTIVE -> E0_REJECTED_FOR_AUTHORITY

If activation is not durably committed, E1 must not be active merely because a node saw preparation or partial evidence. If activation is durably committed, C0 remains historical evidence but fails current authority admission. If recovery cannot establish whether activation committed, current authority is UNKNOWN/QUARANTINED.

## Admission
Verify signature/proof, certificate epoch, predecessor/continuity, authoritative epoch state, and current epoch admissibility. Reject stale certificates after a committed epoch fence while preserving them for historical evidence.

## Race rule
An old certificate arriving before E1 activation may be admitted only if its protected transition linearizes before the activation boundary. A delayed C0 after E1 activation is stale even if cryptographically valid.

ARRIVAL_TIME != AUTHORITY_TIME
SIGNATURE_TIME != LINEARIZATION_TIME
PREPARED != ACTIVE
SEEN_LOCALLY != COMMITTED

## Crash and Byzantine cases
Crash between preparation and activation recovers from the last durable transition boundary. If the boundary is ambiguous, do not guess. Byzantine replay of C0 is blocked by the epoch fence but does not prove C0 never existed. Witnesses signing incompatible activation states produce equivocation and invoke AB104.561/562 conflict handling.

## Nexo consequence
Epoch activation is itself a protected transition with an explicit durable linearization point. The epoch fence belongs in the continuity anchor and recovery boundary. Stale certificates remain auditable evidence but cannot regain authority after a committed fence.
