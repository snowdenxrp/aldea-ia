## Nexo split-brain rule

Two candidate witness epochs may exist as proposals during a partition, but at most one may become `ACTIVE` under the authoritative governance state. A candidate supported only by a partitioned/stale quorum remains non-authoritative.

Candidate state:
`E_old ACTIVE -> E_newA PROPOSED` and `E_newB PROPOSED`

Only a quorum satisfying the protected epoch-transition rule may create:
`ACTIVATION_COMMITTED(E_newX)`.

If two conflicting activation records are observed, the system cannot select one using timestamp, replica freshness, witness count, or arrival order. It enters `QUARANTINE` until an authority with a valid trust basis resolves the conflict.

## Partition semantics

- quorum available on one side -> that side may progress if its epoch-transition conditions are satisfied;
- no valid quorum -> no new activation and no threshold reduction;
- stale minority -> read-only/historical evidence only;
- competing valid-looking epochs -> `UNKNOWN_AUTHORITY` / quarantine until reconciled;
- restored connectivity -> reconcile durable activation history before resuming protected authorization.

This follows the quorum-intersection principle: if two voting configurations can authorize concurrently without a required intersection or joint-transition rule, split-brain authority is possible. ZooKeeper explicitly relies on intersecting quorums. citeturn0search2

## Configuration-transition requirement

Changing witness membership and threshold cannot be treated as an ordinary independent write. The transition must itself be serialized by the authoritative epoch mechanism. A safe candidate is a joint-transition record binding:
`OldEpoch + NewEpoch + OldThreshold + NewThreshold + OldMembers + NewMembers + ParentCheckpoint + TransitionPolicy + ActivationRevision`.

During transition, authorization should require the explicitly defined joint condition; merely seeing both configurations is insufficient.

## Key invariants

`PARTITION != AUTHORITY_LOSS_RECOVERY`
`STALE_QUORUM != CURRENT_AUTHORITY`
`WITNESS_COUNT != QUORUM_VALIDITY`
`CONFLICTING_ACTIVATIONS => QUARANTINE`
`NO_VALID_QUORUM => NO_NEW_ACTIVATION`
`QUORUM_LOSS != SAFETY_THRESHOLD_REDUCTION`
`CHECKPOINT_CONSISTENCY != AUTHORITY_ELECTION`
`MEMBERSHIP_CHANGE_REQUIRES_SERIALIZED_TRANSITION`
`TWO_ACTIVATION_RECORDS_FOR_CONFLICTING_EPOCHS => UNKNOWN_AUTHORITY`
