# Nexo AB104.570 — Dynamic dependency drift during recovery

Date: 2026-09-27
Status: RESEARCH COMPLETE — DESIGN INPUT ONLY

## Evidence
Dynamic Byzantine quorum research shows that changing quorum assumptions while stale configurations remain usable can produce conflicting information unless adjustment rules preserve safe shared-variable semantics. Reconfiguration research likewise requires explicit configuration installation and state transfer; a joining node should not become voting-authoritative until synchronization is complete. citeturn0search7turn0search1turn0search6

## Finding
A recovery quorum is not permanently independent merely because it passed an independence test when recovery began. Dependencies can drift while recovery is in progress: a root may be revoked, a provider may become shared, a key may rotate, a witness may become compromised, or a dependency snapshot may become stale.

Therefore recovery authority must be evaluated against a coherent dependency snapshot at admission and revalidated before the irreversible activation boundary.

## Candidate state machine
RECOVERY_PREPARED
→ DEPENDENCY_SNAPSHOT_CAPTURED
→ RECOVERY_QUORUM_VALIDATED
→ RECOVERY_IN_PROGRESS
→ FINAL_REVALIDATION
→ RECOVERY_AUTHORITY_COMMITTED
→ NEW_AUTHORITY_ACTIVE

A dependency change before FINAL_REVALIDATION invalidates the pending promotion and returns the process to validation/quarantine.

## Critical race
If Root A and Root B are independent at time T0 but both become dependent on Provider X at T1, a certificate assembled from their old evidence must not retain the old independence claim at T1. Historical evidence remains valid for what it actually proved at T0; it cannot silently prove current independence.

Likewise, a recovery certificate prepared before revocation cannot automatically authorize after the revocation boundary.

## Required binding
Recovery certificate should bind:
RecoveryGeneration, DependencySnapshotDigest, WitnessSetEpoch, AuthorityEpoch, ContinuityAnchorDigest, RootMembership, RootStatus/EvidenceEpoch, FenceEpoch, ValidityBoundary.

The final gate recomputes or verifies that the dependency snapshot is still coherent and that no dependency has crossed a revocation/compromise boundary.

## Failure handling
- Drift detected before final commit: invalidate pending certificate; re-evaluate.
- Drift detected after preparation but before activation: no authority promotion.
- Drift discovered after activation: freeze affected authority, invalidate dependent claims, and enter recovery/quarantine as required.
- Drift status UNKNOWN: do not assume independence remains.

## Critical distinctions
FRESH_ROOTS != FRESH_INDEPENDENCE
OLD_INDEPENDENCE_PROOF != CURRENT_INDEPENDENCE
PREPARED_RECOVERY != COMMITTED_RECOVERY
DEPENDENCY_CHANGE != HISTORICAL_ERASURE
REVOCATION != PROOF_OF_PRIOR_INVALIDITY

## Nexo consequence
AB104.570 closes an important temporal dimension of the dependency graph: independence is a versioned, claim-specific property. Recovery must use a coherent dependency snapshot and perform final revalidation immediately before authority activation. This connects the earlier dependency-closure work with witness epochs, recovery generations and continuity anchors.
