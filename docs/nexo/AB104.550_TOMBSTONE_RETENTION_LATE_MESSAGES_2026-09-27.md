# Nexo AB104.550 — Tombstone retention and late messages

Date: 2026-09-27
Status: RESEARCH COMPLETE — DESIGN INPUT ONLY

## Finding
A cancellation/tombstone barrier is only authoritative while the system can still prove that the barrier covers every delayed message that could arrive. Garbage-collecting the tombstone can therefore reopen a replay window unless another durable monotonic barrier, epoch, incarnation, or historical proof replaces it.

## Evidence
Kafka documents that tombstones are retained only for delete.retention.ms and that a lagging consumer can miss them after that interval. Compaction preserves ordering but removes historical records; offsets remain positions, not proof that a removed record never existed. A 2025 Kafka issue also documents stale state after tombstone compaction as a real corner case. citeturn0search0turn0search1turn0search9

## Nexo rule
TOMBSTONE_DELETED != MESSAGE_DID_NOT_EXIST

Safe deletion requires coverage proof that no still-valid delayed delivery can arrive with authority capable of producing the protected effect. If this cannot be proved, retain the old barrier or move the target to a new incarnation/epoch that rejects old messages.

## Candidate lifecycle
ACTIVE_BARRIER -> COVERAGE_WINDOW -> ELIGIBLE_FOR_GC -> GC_COMMITTED

At ELIGIBLE_FOR_GC require evidence for maximum message delay, retry/replay delay, relay/consumer recovery delay, provider idempotency/retention, authority/fence transition, target incarnation transition, and any archival/historical reconciliation path.

## Stronger replacement pattern
Bind obsolete messages to TargetIdentity, TargetIncarnation, AuthorityEpoch, FenceRevision, and EffectID, then advance an authoritative epoch/incarnation. Old messages become structurally stale even after the original tombstone is compacted. This is stronger than wall-clock age alone.

## Critical distinctions
- TOMBSTONE_PRESENT != EFFECT_REVERSED
- TOMBSTONE_DELETED != NONOCCURRENCE
- COMPACTION != HISTORICAL_ERASURE_PROOF
- MESSAGE_OLD != MESSAGE_IMPOSSIBLE
- RETENTION_EXPIRY != EFFECT_NOT_APPLIED
- EPOCH_ADVANCE != RETROACTIVE_CANCELLATION
- TARGET_REINCARNATION != HISTORICAL_ERASURE
- CURRENT_ABSENCE != HISTORICAL_NONOCCURRENCE

## Nexo implication
A cancellation barrier needs an explicit RetentionCoverage contract. Garbage collection is a protected transition whenever the retained artifact participates in stale-message rejection or negative-effect proof. If coverage is UNKNOWN, keep the barrier or quarantine the affected target/effect domain.
