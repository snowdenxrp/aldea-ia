Durable receipt archival must preserve the original provider receipt bytes and bind them to the exact semantic context: `OperationID + EffectID + RequestDigest + TargetIdentity + TargetIncarnation + ProviderIdentity + ProviderRevision + AuthorityEpoch + DecisionDigest + FenceRevision`.

## Archival binding
Candidate:
`ArchiveBindingDigest = H(Domain || ProtocolVersion || OperationID || EffectID || RequestDigest || TargetIdentity || TargetIncarnation || ProviderIdentity || ProviderRevision || AuthorityEpoch || DecisionDigest || FenceRevision || ReceiptDigest)`

A valid receipt must not be reusable for another operation, target incarnation, provider scope, authority epoch, or effect class. A digest without preserved receipt material cannot reconstruct the receipt.

## Retention
Retention classes remain design categories: `R0` transient, `R1` operation-lifetime, `R2` protected historical, `R3` long-term continuity. Provider pruning must not silently destroy a claim if Nexo has preserved sufficient authenticated evidence; otherwise the historical claim may become UNKNOWN.

## Archive trust boundary
The archive stores evidence; it does not become an authority source. Recovery of an old archive checkpoint is not authorization to roll back. Provider migration creates a new evidence statement rather than rebinding old receipts to a new provider.
