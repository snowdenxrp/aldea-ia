# Nexo AB104.558 — Witness partition and split-brain anchor activation

Date: 2026-09-27
Status: RESEARCH COMPLETE — DESIGN INPUT ONLY

## Evidence
Raft's joint-consensus protocol requires majorities from both old and new configurations during membership transition; neither configuration may make unilateral decisions. The Raft paper explicitly identifies direct old->new switching as unsafe because it can create two independent majorities. etcd's learner design likewise keeps a new member non-voting until it has caught up and is explicitly promoted. citeturn0search25turn0search5

## Finding
For Nexo witness-set rotation, a network partition must never let W_old and W_new independently activate competing anchor histories. The minimum safety rule is intersection-based authorization across the transition, not mere reachability or signature validity.

Candidate joint state:
W_JOINT = {W_old, W_new, epoch_transition_id}

During W_JOINT, protected anchor activation requires the configured authority threshold from BOTH witness sets, subject to the independence/failure-domain policy. If either side cannot establish the required cross-set condition, it may observe and store candidates but cannot promote one as authoritative.

## Partition cases
1. Partition isolates enough W_old to satisfy old-only threshold but not W_new: old-only activation is rejected.
2. Partition isolates enough W_new to satisfy new-only threshold but not W_old: new-only activation is rejected.
3. Each side has its own local signatures but no cross-set proof: both remain non-authoritative/QUARANTINED.
4. Partition occurs after W_new becomes fully active: W_old cannot reactivate itself; W_new follows its normal quorum rules.
5. Partition occurs before joint activation is committed: old configuration remains current only if its own protected authority state says so; the prepared new set cannot self-promote.

## Important nuance
The exact threshold cannot be reduced to a fixed numeric quorum. The witness sets must be evaluated for independence, correlated failure domains, compromise assumptions, and the claim being protected. A numerical majority can be unsafe if both sets share a common dependency.

## Anchor succession
A successor anchor must prove:
PreviousAnchorDigest -> SuccessorAnchorDigest
and bind the active WitnessSetEpoch + AuthorityEpoch + continuity counter. A locally valid signature on a successor that lacks a valid predecessor relationship is not sufficient.

## Recovery
If a partition heals and competing candidates exist, Nexo should not choose by local timestamp or wall-clock freshness. It should reconcile using the highest independently justified continuity boundary and quarantine conflicting histories when no safe ordering can be established.

## Nexo consequence
Witness-set rotation requires a joint epoch with explicit transition semantics. During that epoch, no partitioned subset can activate a competing continuity history. This is the witness analogue of joint consensus. The witness set itself becomes a protected versioned dependency and cannot silently shrink because nodes disappear.
